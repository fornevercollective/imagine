The preset is designed so the video output matches the motion and holographic quality of the two reference videos while using Product Showcase framing.

For the example headshot used here: The initial preset run used a fixed Neanderthal stage (7) for dramatic effect. This created the size/shape mismatch you noticed — Neanderthal crania are long and low with heavy brow and occipital bun, very different from most modern heads.

Improved preset behavior (now in the prompts above):

  • Grok first analyzes the headshot photo to extract real skull dimensions (ratios, brow, jaw, overall shape).
  • The skull is morphed to exactly fit those measurements from the photo (same width, height, proportions as your visible head).
  • Then accurate hominin fossil traits (from the timeline JSON + Bone Clones/Smithsonian refs) are blended on top of the photo-matched skull.
  • You (or the prompt) can specify a stage or let it "auto-estimate best fitting influence" based on visible features (strong brow/jaw → Neanderthal lean; high forehead/gracile → sapiens lean).

Result: the skull "belongs" to the person in the photo while still showing real evolutionary bone structure. Re-generate using the updated prompt.txt in this folder for better matching on your headshots.
